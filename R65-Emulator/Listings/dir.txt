  13     4 Display the directory of a disk drive.
  14     4 Uses EPROM (disk.asm) calls to get info
  15     4 from disk directory.
  16     4 
  17     4 Makes a table to find out how long the
  18     4 longest name is. Then computes the number
  19     4 of columns which can be displayed and
  20     4 displays the directory.
  21     4 
  22     4 option /S sorts the directory
  23     4        /F full directory date
  24     4        /D include deleted files
  25     4 
  26     4 Usage:
  27     4   DIR drive [/options]
  28     4      where drive is the drive number 1 (default) or 0
  29     4   DIR diskname [/options]
  30     4      where disknamename is the name of a floppy disk
  31     4 
  32     4 The directory table has 256 entries of 32 bytes
  33     4 The disk name is stored in the last entry (255)
  34     4 The last currently used entry has filtyp=TEND
  35     4 }
